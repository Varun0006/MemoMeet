# Problem Statement Selected

## Meeting Preparation Agent

Meetings often create important information, but that information is easy to lose after the meeting ends.

During a meeting, people make decisions, commit to tasks, share preferences, raise concerns, and discuss future plans. These details are usually stored in long notes, transcripts, chat messages, or someone's memory. Over time, they become difficult to find and even easier to forget.

This creates a common problem for community leaders, organizers, managers, and teams:

- Previous decisions are forgotten.
- Follow-ups are missed.
- Action items are not tracked properly.
- Participant preferences are lost.
- The same topics are discussed again and again.
- Every meeting starts from zero instead of continuing from the last conversation.

For example, if a participant promised to contact sponsors in the previous meeting, the organizer should remember to follow up before or during the next meeting. Without a memory system, that commitment can easily disappear.

MemoMeet solves this problem by acting as a persistent memory layer for meetings.
